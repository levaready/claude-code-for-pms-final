## What ties these together

The biggest risk right now is chasing the loudest signal instead of the largest one. Row 2 says the tickets are noisy; row 7 says the interviews are a non-representative sample. Row 1 says the CSV, which is quieter than both, points at something worse: total silence for four responders, and even the two piles combined only give a qualitative account for half of them. Row 4 closes off the easy explanation everyone (including Marcus and Priya) reaches for first. Severity is also subjective across sources (row 3) — one calm handler shouldn't be read as evidence nothing's wrong. That leaves rows 1 and 3 as the two things actually worth Wen and Ravi's time next, and both need daily-grain data to resolve, not more interviews.

## Least effort, most impact

Of the six, **row 2 is the cheapest to move and the highest-leverage**: one clarifying question to Ravi (what `pings_taken` measures, and whether it reconciles with what handlers see) settles a dependency every other row sits on. Row 1 has the highest business impact but unknown effort until diagnosed. See `analysis/asks-drafts.md` for the drafted question.
