Invoice Reminders — one-pager
Product, Billing
28 September 2026

Problem

Customers on net-30 terms pay late 22% of the time, and most of the late
ones say nobody reminded them. Finance chases these by hand and estimates it
costs about 14 hours a week. Late payments also push our cash forecast out
by roughly two weeks.

Proposal

Send an automatic email reminder five days before an invoice is due and
another two days after it is overdue.

Owner

To be decided once the quarter's roadmap is set.

Success measure

Late payments on net-30 invoices fall from 22% to under 15% within one
quarter of release.

Scope

Two reminder emails on net-30 invoices. No text messages, no changes to
payment terms, no customer portal.
