Dashboard Export — one-pager
Product, Analytics
1 October 2026

Problem

Customers who want their dashboard numbers in a spreadsheet copy them out
by hand. We get about 40 support tickets a month asking for a data export,
and two enterprise renewals this quarter listed it as a blocker.

Proposal

Let customers export any dashboard to CSV and PDF from the dashboard menu.

Owner

Wei Zhang, PM for Analytics.

Success measure

Support tickets asking for a data export fall from about 40 a month to
under 10 within two months of release.

Scope

CSV only. PDF is out of scope for this brief.

Next steps

Design to start on PDF page layouts this week. Engineering to estimate CSV
and PDF export together so they can ship in one release.
