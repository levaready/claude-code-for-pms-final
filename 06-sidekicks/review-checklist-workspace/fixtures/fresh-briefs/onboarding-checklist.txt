Onboarding Checklist — one-pager
Product, Growth
30 September 2026

Problem

New accounts often stall in the first week. Of the accounts created in
August, 38% never connected their first data source, and those accounts
churn at three times the rate of the others. Customer success hears the
same thing in calls: people do not know what to do first.

Proposal

Add a short checklist on the home screen for the first 14 days: connect a
data source, invite a teammate, create a first report. Each step ticks off
as it is completed.

Owner

Jamal Rivera, PM for Growth.

Success measure

We will know it is working when customers feel more confident about getting
started and onboarding feels smoother.

Scope

The checklist on the home screen for new accounts only. No changes to the
sign-up flow, no emails, no changes for existing accounts.
