Saved Searches for the Agent Inbox — one-pager
Product, Support Tools
21 September 2026

Problem

Support agents re-type the same filters dozens of times a day: "billing,
open, waiting on us, last 7 days". In the August time study, agents spent
about 9% of each shift rebuilding filters, and the three most senior agents
named it as the first thing they would fix. New agents get it wrong more
often, which is why some tickets sit in the wrong queue for hours.

Proposal

Let an agent save any filter combination in the inbox with a name, and pick
it from a list the next time. Saved searches belong to the agent who made
them.

Owner

Priya Nair, PM for Support Tools. Engineering lead: Tomas Ek.

Success measure

Median time from opening the inbox to the first reply on a filtered queue
drops from 6.1 minutes to under 4 minutes within eight weeks of release. We
will also track the share of tickets that land in the wrong queue (7% today).

Scope

Saved searches for the agent inbox only, private to each agent. No sharing
between agents, no admin view, no saved searches in the reporting tool.
Those may be follow-ups but are not part of this brief.
