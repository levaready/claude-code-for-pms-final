Smart Notifications — one-pager
Product, Mobile
2 October 2026

Proposal

We will build smart notifications that learn when each user is most likely
to read a message and deliver it then, instead of the moment it is
generated. The system will use each person's recent open times and rank
pending notifications so the most useful one goes first.

Problem

Users ignore most of what we send. Last month 71% of active users opened no
notification at all, and the number of people who turned notifications off
has doubled since March.

Owner

Aisha Khan, PM for Mobile.

Success measure

The share of active users who open at least one notification a week rises
from 29% to 40% within three months of release.

Scope

Delivery timing and ordering of existing notification types only. No new
notification types and no changes to the settings screen.
