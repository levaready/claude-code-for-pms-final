How this works
  • Every hero gets offers (calls for help) from Dispatch. The file tells us, for each hero and week, how many offers were sent and how many the hero took.
  • Acceptance rate is offers taken divided by offers sent, for everyone added together. It is a good number for "are heroes saying yes?" It cannot tell if some heroes stopped getting offers at all.
  • Quiet count is how many heroes are left out this week. It counts people, not offers. One hero going quiet moves it from 0 to 1, even when the rate barely moves.
  • Rule A uses one fixed bar (fewer than N offers). Rule B compares each hero to their own usual, so it fits heroes who normally get few offers and heroes who normally get many.
  • "Weeks after release" counts from the week of 10 Aug, which holds the 12 Aug release. The week of 17 Aug is 1 week after.
  • "False alarm" here means a hero flagged who is not one of the four (Farlight, Meteor Mite, The Undertow, Vesper) that the team found in this file. That list comes from the file itself: it is the group whose offers fell to 0 to 2 a week and stayed there.
  • Everything on this page is worked out in the page from the 160 rows of the call history file. Nothing is typed in by hand except the list of four names.
