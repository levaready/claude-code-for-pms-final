# Simulated hero interviews: testing the Fix Options page

5 Oct 2026. Five heroes were interviewed about the Fix Options prototype (first version, before the changes listed at the end).

## Read this first

- **These are simulated.** Each hero was played by a separate agent, so the answers are rehearsal and a source of ideas to test. They are not evidence about real responders.
- **The agents share one underlying model,** so they agree more than real people would.
- **Invented details.** Daily-life details (a bus, gloves, a phone in a bag) were made up. Each agent listed what it invented.
- **The page leaned on them.** Its headline named option D as the one that works best, which probably pushed the four stuck heroes toward D.
- **Wrong audience.** Fix Options is built for Helen and Wen, not for heroes. The results say more about how heroes want to be told things than about the model.
- **No privacy issues.** Each hero read only their own weekly numbers, their own tickets if any, and the page. No interviews were read.

## How it was run

Each hero got the same 12 open questions. None names a favorite option or asks "would this help?". Part A came before they saw the page. They saw the page for Part B.

**Part A (before the page)**
1. Tell me about the last time an offer came in and you weren't able to answer it. What happened?
2. When an offer arrives, what goes through your head? What decides whether you take it, and what gets in the way of answering fast?
3. What, if anything, do you know about how the app decides who gets offers?
4. Think about the last few weeks. How has the number of offers you get felt to you, and what do you think explains it? What have you done about it, if anything?

**Part B (with the page)**
5. Take a minute to look it over, then tell me in your own words what this page is for and what it's showing.
6. What, if anything, is confusing, missing, or hard to believe?
7. There are four options, A to D. Which one, if any, would matter most in your own situation, and which would matter least? Why?
8. Is there anything about any of the options that worries you, or that could make things worse for you or for other heroes?
9. Pick whichever option you like. If the company put it in place tomorrow, what would you notice in an ordinary week, and how would you know it was working or not?
10. What would you change about this page, or add to it, so it was useful to someone in your position?

**Part C**
11. What would you need to know or see before you'd trust that a fix like this would work for you?
12. Is there anything we didn't ask that we should have?

The heroes were Meteor Mite, Vesper, The Undertow and Farlight (all left out) and The Gale (busy). The temperaments were my assumptions.

|---|---|

## Meteor Mite (left out, handler Kip)

**Basis:** 10–12 offers a week and 6–9 taken through 3 Aug. Then 10 sent / 4 taken (10 Aug), 4/1, 2/0, 1/0. The drop gets worse every week and never recovers.

**Q1.** Honestly it's been so long since one came in that I can't say. The one I remember was in the middle of August. I was out on a bus and I saw it buzz about when the clock was running down, and by the time I got the mask on and the app open it was gone. I'd have taken it. Since then there hasn't really been anything to miss.

**Q2.** I think "can I do this one right now?" I look at how far it is and whether I'm in a state to go. Mostly I'd say yes, I took most of them before. What gets in the way is being somewhere I can't pull the phone out, like on transit or in a shop, or with the mask half on. It's a short window and I'm not always holding the phone.

**Q3.** Nothing really. I assumed it picks whoever's close and free. I've never seen it explained. If there's a score or a list, nobody told me.

**Q4.** It went from a steady few a week to almost nothing, and the last couple of weeks it's about one or none. I wonder if the app forgot about me, or put me down as someone who doesn't answer. I haven't done much. I checked my settings, made sure I was marked available, and then I just waited. I'm not going to chase an app about it.

**Q5.** It looks like the company's trying out ways to fix heroes who stopped getting offers. You can change some settings and see how many heroes stay stuck. The big message seems to be that putting the time back to 90 seconds alone doesn't help much, but doing that plus a one-time reset helps a lot. It's made-up numbers though, so it's more a test than an answer.

**Q6.** "Score" and "healing" and "stuck" I mostly got from context. I don't know what "the middle (0.5)" means to me as a person. The heroes are made up, so I can't tell if any of it is me. "Heroes say yes 90 to 99 percent of the time when they have time" is a bit hard to believe, since I took more like 70 percent. It also never says when anything would really happen, or whether anyone has decided to do it. And there's a lot of small print.

**Q7.** D matters most to me. It's the only one where the example heroes who were down to 0 to 2 offers jump back up, like Hero D to 14 and Hero M to 10. That's what I'd need, because waiting hasn't done anything. A matters least, since it's just leaving things as they are, which is what's been happening to me. B on its own looks nearly the same as doing nothing for the stuck ones.

**Q8.** The reset gives a lot of people a push at once, and I wonder if it takes offers away from the ones who've been fine. It also says Supply might read this, and I don't know what that means for me, so maybe my gear gets scheduled strangely. And with 90 seconds back, I don't know if that means more waiting for the people running the call. I also worry about a one-time reset, because what happens if I drop again afterwards?

**Q9.** With D I'd expect my phone to start buzzing again, maybe several times that first week. I'd know it worked if I got offers in the range I had in July, about 10 a week. If I got one or two again after a couple of weeks, I'd know it hadn't worked for me. I'd probably only trust it once I had a normal full week.

**Q10.** Put a plain line at the top saying "if you're getting almost no offers, here's what we think happened and what we're doing." Add a date. I'd also want the real four heroes, or at least a way to see my own line, instead of Hero D and Hero M. And a short note on what "stuck" means in everyday words.

**Q11.** That someone checked it against real people and not made-up ones. I'd want to hear that it's been tried on a few real heroes, and that there's a way to tell them if it didn't work. Also an actual date and someone I can ask. A page that says "we haven't asked Wen" doesn't make me feel sure.

**Q12.** Whether anyone's told the heroes who stopped getting offers that this is a known problem. I didn't file anything, because I assumed it was me. I think a lot of people who've gone quiet aren't complaining, they're just drifting away.

**Where I was confused or stopped reading:** "Score", "heal", "the middle (0.5)", "draws", "average of 10 runs" (skimmed or guessed). The long assumptions section (stopped at "Not checked"). The "Supply" and "availability record" line. What "stuck" means as a number (under 3 offers) until tied to my own weeks.

**Details I invented:** the quiet, discouraged, practical temperament (assumed); the mid-August miss on a bus with the mask half on; being on transit or in shops and unable to reach the phone; checking settings and availability, then waiting; not filing a ticket because I assumed it was my fault; believing the app picks whoever's close and free; "took more like 70 percent" (loosely from the CSV); the worry about Supply scheduling my gear and about the reset taking offers from others.

**The one thing I most want the team to know:** Heroes like me go quiet without complaining, because we assume the app dropped us for something we did. Please tell us it's a known problem, give a date, and check any fix against real heroes, not made-up ones.

## Vesper (left out, handler Aunt Dot)

**Basis:** 13–15 offers a week and 10–13 accepted through 3 Aug, about 80%. 12 sent / 6 accepted in the week of 10 Aug. Then 5, 2 and 1 offers, with 1, 0 and 0 accepted.

**Q1.** It was one of the few that came in during the middle of August. The phone went off while I was in the middle of something with both hands busy. By the time I'd got to it, it was gone. **Q3.** Not much. I assumed it picks whoever's close and free and can do the job. I never looked into it. I do things when they come, and I don't know what happens in the background.

**Q4.** It's gone from busy to nearly nothing. I used to get a couple every other day, and lately it's about one a week, and some weeks none. My best guess is that something changed on the company's side, because I haven't changed how I work. I've mentioned it to Aunt Dot and I've kept my phone on me more. Nothing has changed. **Q5.** It looks like a page where the team tests four ideas for getting heroes like me more offers again. It uses made-up heroes and shows what the offers per week would look like under each idea. **Q6.** "Score" is confusing. It reads like a grade somebody gave me, and I didn't know I had one. "Stuck" is also strange. I'd say I'm not getting offers, and the page says stuck. **Q7.** D matters most, since it lifts the low scores and gives people a bit more time, and it's the only one where the made-up heroes get a real number of offers back. A matters least because it's just "do nothing", which is what's happening to me now. B is the one I'd have guessed at, but the page says it only helps a little. C looks too slow. **Q8.** Yes. If the time goes back to 90 seconds, offers sit around longer, and it might slow things down for whoever needs help. **Q9.** I'll take D. I'd notice the phone going off again, a few a day, not one a week. I'd know it worked if I was back near 12 or 14 offers a week and I could take most of them. If a month went by and I was still seeing one or two, I'd know it hadn't. I'd just count them. I don't need a chart.

**Q10.** I'd put in plain words what each term means. I'd also show real heroes' numbers, since you have them, not the pretend ones. And I'd say clearly how many weeks until a fix would show up for someone who's been quiet since August. I'd leave out the sliders, and a lot of the page I'd just skip.

**Q11.** I'd want to know it was tried on real heroes and that someone looked at the people who dropped off, not just the average. I'd want to know it was checked with whoever handles the other side of it. And I'd want someone to actually tell me what changed, because nobody's told me anything so far.

**Q12.** You didn't ask if I'm still willing to take offers. I am. I'd also have liked a question about how I found out something was wrong. I only found out because the offers dried up, and nobody told me.

**Where I was confused or stopped reading:** "Score" and "stuck" were new words to me, and I didn't know I had a score. "Average of 10 runs", "draw set" and the sliders (skimmed and stopped reading). The "Assumptions" section mostly passed me by, apart from the line about another team. Not sure why the four example heroes were chosen, or what the dotted line meant at first.

**Details I invented:** the matter-of-fact, proud, not-a-complainer temperament (assumed); hands busy and the phone in another room; mentioning it to Aunt Dot and keeping the phone closer; the offer-count descriptions in Q4 are rough readings of the CSV; not knowing about a score and assuming the app picks "close, free and capable" people; willingness to keep taking offers.

**The one thing I most want the team to know:** I didn't change anything, and I was reliable, and then the offers just stopped. Nobody told me, so please tell people like me what happened and when it's getting fixed.
